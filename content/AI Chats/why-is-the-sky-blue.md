::: user
Why is the sky blue? And if blue light scatters the most, why are sunsets orange instead of even bluer?
:::

::: assistant
Both come from **Rayleigh scattering**. Air molecules scatter short wavelengths far more strongly:

$$I \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$$

Blue (~450 nm) scatters about **4.4×** more than red (~650 nm), so scattered blue light reaches you from every direction.

At sunset, light passes through ~38× more air, so most of the blue is scattered *out* of the beam before it reaches you. What's left is red and orange.
:::

::: note
The 38× figure is the air mass at the horizon. Checked, and it's right.
:::
