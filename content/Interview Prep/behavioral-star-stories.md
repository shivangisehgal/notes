::: info Format
**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult. Keep each to under 2 minutes, and make the result a number.
:::

::: qa Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
**S:** We planned to ship a feature without load testing.
**T:** I owned the payments service it touched.
**A:** I ran a one-day load test and shared the p99 latency graph instead of arguing.
**R:** We delayed by 3 days, fixed a connection-pool limit, and launched with zero incidents.
:::

::: qa Tell me about a failure.
Pick something real, own it in one sentence, and spend most of the time on what you changed afterwards.
:::
